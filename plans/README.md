## Evidence behind these plans

From one Mac on 2026-11-14: six scheduled skill-only runs reclaimed ~0.3 GiB
in total; one script-driven run retired 115 finished T3 worktrees and moved
free space from 5.4 GiB to 90 GiB. Review of the prototype scripts found the
safety issues plans 002 and 004 address (name-only "generated" matching, stale
ownership/process data during a long loop, a removal timeout, results written
only at the end, no overlap lock).
